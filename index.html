Module 01 · Territory: Kakinada

AI-HCP Targeting & ranking

Built from primary RCPA field research across Kakinada's ophthalmology base — Nayana Eye Care, Srikiran Eye Hospital, Vasan Eye Care Hospital, SK Superspeciality Hospital, Apollo Hospitals and independent practice. Each HCP's weekly glaucoma patient volume and stated MISOPT preference were captured directly; the model converts that into a priority score and an action tag — retain-and-grow vs. conversion target — live as you re-weight the signals.

Module 02

Prescriber shift detection

Genesis → MIS ingests monthly IQVIA + territory sales data continuously. When a prescriber's run-rate breaks its trend, the model raises an alert and attaches a recovery playbook — immediate scientific detailing, additional samples, a CME invite within 7 days, and a scheduled follow-up.

Module 03

AI demand forecasting

Projects prescriber-level demand 6–8 weeks out from trailing Rx velocity and seasonality, so distributor inventory lands before expected demand — not after a stock-out has already cost you share.

Module 04

Personalized doctor detailing

One customized digital detail per doctor — built from specialty, patient profile and prior prescribing — instead of the same generic deck for every HCP on the list.

Module 05

WhatsApp patient bot

Automated, timed touchpoints keyed to real adherence risk — a refill reminder before the drop runs out, a withdrawal check when therapy lapses, and Patient Assistance Program support on request.

Module 06

AI social listening (NLP)

NLP monitors patient forums, glaucoma Facebook groups, X, HCP communities and doctor discussion boards. The model classifies each mention as an adverse event, a patient preference, a competitor perception, or an emerging treatment need.

Combined with IQVIA data + CRM + Dr. Rx + pharmacy sales, this becomes one closed-loop view of demand, sentiment and safety.

Module 07 · Territory: Kakinada

RCPA — retail chemist audit

Prescription data shows intent; RCPA shows what actually left the shelf. 20 Kakinada pharmacies audited across MISOPT and three direct competitors — Dorzox, Dortas, Dorsun. Chemists are tiered by total glaucoma Rx volume, and audit frequency follows tier.
